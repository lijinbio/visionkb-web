Before you use the chat

  • Your questions, this tab's chat history and the tool results (VisionKB facts and HECA atlas summary values; HECA is lab-access data) are sent to Anthropic under your own account with the key you enter.
  • The key stays in this browser tab (sessionStorage). It is sent only to this server, in one header, for the call it makes to Anthropic, and is never stored or logged there. Forget key removes it at once; closing the tab does too.
  • Answers come from the knowledge graph through tools, cite the release and evidence, and can still be wrong: check the linked evidence.
